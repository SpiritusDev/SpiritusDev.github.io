My name is Sergio Ninstant, and I’m a senior in Gaming and Multimedia. In my free time, I like to write fantasy stories, listen to music or audiobooks and play games. When I was around the age of four I played my first ever video game on the Nintendo Wii, Super Mario Bros.Wii. I was completely drawn into the world of Super Mario. I didn't know exactly what I was doing, but I was having fun and that changed my life forever. When I was twelve, I wanted to become a game developer or at least some job that involved coding. But at that age, it seemed so daunting to try and pick up any form of coding, plus I barely even knew what coding was at the time.

Fast forward to my sophomore year of high school I was selected to go to something called Questar III and one of the programs they offered was Gaming and Multimedia. I had to make a decision: take on the extra curricular activity which I knew was going to be hard but would set me on a path that I knew I wanted or just stay at my home school playing it safe. Through all of my indecision I knew there was only one answer I would truly go with.

In my junior year of high school I was accepted into the Questar III Gaming and Multimedia program. Here, I met some amazing people that became good friends, and I created my first ever game with a game engine called Construct 3. At the time, I was not able to finish the game, what the game was supposed to be was a horde killing rougelite but it was to hard for me to create with the timeframe we had so I just created a smaller version where the player fights a few goblins and then a giant blob called Muc. I created all the assets besides the goblins for the game. For our final project, my group and I created a game called Eldritch Incident: 1948. We definitely bit off more than we could chew, but it was a great learning experience. The game was supposed to be a turn-based game where players control four characters, and it is set in 1948 right after World War II, where a virus from another world took over most of the population. Players fight everything from zombies to eldritch gods. I was the lead programmer and did most of the coding myself.

Looking ahead, I plan to use these games to look back to see how far I’ve come.
